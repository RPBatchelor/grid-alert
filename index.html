Outage data

Read from each distributor's public outage tracker via the app's server. These are undocumented public feeds and may change without notice. Dots show the most recent fetch.

  • AusNet — Outage Tracker
    Live AusNet planned and unplanned outages
    outagetracker.com.au · feed
  • Powercor & CitiPower — Live outage map
    Live outages for both networks (one shared feed)
    powercor.com.au · feed
  • Jemena — Electricity outages
    Live Jemena outages
    jemena.com.au · feed
  • United Energy — Outage map
    Live United Energy outages
    unitedenergy.com.au · feed

Weather

  • Open-Meteo
    Forecast grid: wind, gusts, rain, temperature (CC BY 4.0)
    open-meteo.com
  • RainViewer
    Observed rain radar overlay
    rainviewer.com
  • Windy Map Forecast API (optional)
    Alternative weather base map when a key is configured
    api.windy.com

Fire danger

  • CFA — Total Fire Bans & Fire Danger Ratings forecast
    Rating and Total Fire Ban status per fire district, today + 4 days (BoM forecast)
    cfa.vic.gov.au · RSS feed
  • CFA — Fire restriction dates (Fire Danger Period)
    Declaration start/end dates per municipality (read from the CFA web page; no feed is published)
    cfa.vic.gov.au
  • Vicmap Lite — Local Government Areas
    Council boundaries for the Fire Danger Period layer (DataVic open WFS, CC BY), simplified
    DataVic
  • Vicmap Admin — CFA Total Fire Ban District polygons
    Fire district boundaries (DataVic open WFS, CC BY), simplified for display
    DataVic

Disclaimer

Decision support only, not a source of truth. Outage data comes from public trackers and may be delayed or incomplete; weather values are model forecasts, not observations or Bureau of Meteorology warnings; network boundaries are approximate. Always confirm with network control systems and official warnings.

Integrations

Windy

With a Windy Map Forecast API key, the base map switches to Windy (ECMWF, ICON and GFS models; gusts, rain, thunder, temperature). A Windy Premium subscription does not include this key. Get one at api.windy.com/keys. Stored only in this browser.
